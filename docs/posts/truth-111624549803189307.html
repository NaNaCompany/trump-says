Alexander Talel, CNN: “To ignore the injustice of the law as applied to Trump is to welcome its unfair application to us all...The commercial consequences of the law are disturbingly obvious and potentially devastating. Apparently. What about a deli owner claiming in ads to have the ‘best sandwiches in the world’? Could the AG go after that owner for ‘fraud’ even though customers have only praised and never complained about the quality of those sandwiches?”
