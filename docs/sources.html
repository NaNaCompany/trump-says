Timestamps

Timezone-aware source timestamps are normalized to UTC and displayed in US Eastern time, Korea time or UTC. IANA timezones handle daylight saving time. For 823 records, the source time differed by more than two seconds from the creation time encoded in the post ID. These records use the ID timestamp, retain the original source timestamp and carry a correction label.

X Snowflake ID documentation. Older X IDs that cannot be checked this way retain their source timestamp.

Normalization & missing records

  • Posts are deduplicated by platform and post ID. IDs remain decimal strings to avoid precision loss.
  • HTML is converted to plain text. Reversible UTF-8 / Latin-1 decoding errors are repaired; raw snapshots remain unchanged. When source texts differ, the longest complete source version is retained without combining texts. Variants and source hashes are recorded locally.
  • Original, repost, quote and deletion labels come from the source. Unsupported classifications remain unknown. A deletion flag is not a live status check.
  • Original links may require sign-in or be unavailable after deletion or due to regional restrictions.
  • Text within images, video or audio is not searchable unless it appears in the archived post text.
  • Uncollected deleted posts, earlier revisions and other social accounts are missing. X coverage ends at the last source timestamp shown above.
  • This is a snapshot. Automatic live synchronization is not configured.

Home feed and like snapshots

The home page shows up to 100 collected records ordered by known source like counts, then the remaining eligible records newest first through local HTML pages. Native reposts are excluded from the home/feed and displayed month/day lists. Unknown source classifications remain marked unknown. Like values are historical source snapshots, not live counts; missing values remain unavailable rather than zero. New feed bodies display through official platform embeds. Existing detail/search text and source records are unchanged.

Like snapshot provenance and the fixed source selection policy are recorded in data/likes.json. Its capture timestamp is the local source collection time, not an individual count measurement time.
